About RR

The Rajasthan Royals (RR) are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Jaipur, Rajasthan that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Founded in 2008 as one of the original eight franchises, the team is owned by Manoj Badale and The Royals Sports Group.

The team plays its home matches at the Sawai Mansingh Stadium in Jaipur and also at the Assam Cricket Association Stadium in Guwahati.
