About DC

The Delhi Capitals, formerly known as the Delhi Daredevils, are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Delhi that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

The franchise is owned by GMR Group and JSW Sports. Their home ground is the Arun Jaitley Stadium in New Delhi.

The team reached their first IPL final in 2020 against the Mumbai Indians.
