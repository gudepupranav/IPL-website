About PK

The Punjab Kings (PBKS), formerly known as Kings XI Punjab, are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in New Chandigarh, Punjab that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL).

The franchise is jointly owned by Mohit Burman, Ness Wadia, Preity Zinta, and Karan Paul. The team plays its home matches at the Maharaja Yadavindra Singh International Cricket Stadium, and also occasionally at Dharamshala and Indore.

The team reached the IPL final in 2014 but finished as runners-up after losing to Kolkata Knight Riders. They also finished runners-up in the 2025 IPL season.
