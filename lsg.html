About LSG

The Lucknow Super Giants (LSG) are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL). The franchise is owned by the RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group. Ekana Cricket Stadium is its home ground. As of 2025, the team is coached by Justin Langer and captained by Rishabh Pant.

Since their first appearance in 2022, the team has reached the playoffs in two seasons (2022 and 2023) and finished 7th in the points table in 2024 and 2025. Currently, LSG are the only active team to have never reached an IPL final.
