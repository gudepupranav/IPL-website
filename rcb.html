About RCB

Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB), formerly Royal Challengers Bangalore, are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Bengaluru, Karnataka that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Founded in 2008 by United Spirits, the team's home ground is the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium.

RCB won their first IPL title in 2025. The team previously finished as runners-up in 2009, 2011, and 2016. They have qualified for the playoffs in several seasons and are one of the most popular IPL franchises.

As of 2025, the team is captained by Rajat Patidar and coached by Andy Flower.
