About MI

The Mumbai Indians are a professional Twenty20 cricket team based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, that competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL). The team was founded in 2008 and is owned by Reliance Industries through its subsidiary Indiawin Sports. The team plays its home matches at the Wankhede Stadium. Mumbai Indians is one of the most successful teams in IPL history.
